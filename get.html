Installing

  • iPhone/iPad: tap the button above, install TestFlight if prompted, then install Lift Progress from inside TestFlight.
  • Android: tap "Step 1" above to join the testers group (one time only), then tap "Step 2," tap "Become a tester," and install from the Play Store page.
  • Future updates show up automatically through TestFlight or the Play Store — no need to come back to this page again.

Sending feedback

On iPhone/iPad: use TestFlight's built-in feedback button — it lets you screenshot and mark up the issue right in the app, and it comes straight to us.

On Android: use our feedback form — takes a minute, screenshots welcome.

The standout feature

A plate calculator that knows your gym.

Most plate calculators stop at arithmetic. Lift Progress also considers the equipment you actually own and the loading rules for the lift.

  • Tracks iron and bumper plates separately
  • Uses your actual plate inventory
  • Applies lift-specific loading constraints
  • Returns physically achievable combinations

Ready before the first rep

Automatic warm-ups without the mental math.

Set the working weight and Lift Progress builds the warm-up ladder, then shows the exact plates needed for each step based on your inventory.

  • Percentage-based warm-up schemes
  • Fully editable before or during the workout
  • Exact per-side plate guidance
  • Saved with the workout for future reference

Private by design

Your training data stays on your device.

Lift Progress does not require an account and currently has no Lift Progress-operated backend, analytics, ads, or third-party data collection. Your workout data is stored locally and only leaves your device when you choose to export it.

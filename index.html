Lift Progress Privacy Policy Effective Date: September 1, 2026 Last Updated: September 1, 2026 Lift Progress is a workout planning and logging application developed by David Burton. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Lift Progress mobile application. Lift Progress is designed with privacy in mind. The app does not require an account and does not use a Lift Progress-operated server or cloud service to store your workout data. Information you enter into the app is stored locally on your device. 1. Information You Enter Into Lift Progress Lift Progress allows you to enter and store information used to plan, log, and review your strength-training activities. Depending on how you use the app, this information may include: workout plans and workout history; exercises, sets, repetitions, and weights; bodyweight entries; equipment and weight-plate inventory; warm-up and plate-calculation information; personal records and progress information; and app preferences and settings. This information is processed and stored locally on your device so that Lift Progress can provide its features. David Burton does not receive, collect, or have access to this locally stored information through Lift Progress. 2. Information We Collect Lift Progress does not currently collect or transmit personal information or app usage data to David Burton or to a Lift Progress-operated server. Lift Progress does not currently use: user accounts; a Lift Progress backend or cloud database; advertising; advertising identifiers; analytics services; third-party tracking technologies; or developer-operated remote storage of your workout information. Lift Progress does not sell your personal information or workout information. Lift Progress does not use your information for advertising or cross-app tracking. 3. Local Data Storage Your Lift Progress data is stored locally on your device. Because Lift Progress does not operate an account or cloud-sync system, David Burton generally cannot access, retrieve, restore, or remotely delete data stored by the app on your device. Your device's operating system or services you separately enable may provide device-level backup, transfer, or restoration functionality. Those services are controlled by the applicable platform or service provider and are not Lift Progress-operated cloud storage. 4. Backup and Export Lift Progress includes functionality that allows you to export a backup of your app data in JSON format. Exporting a backup is initiated and controlled by you. Lift Progress does not automatically upload exported backup files to David Burton or to a Lift Progress-operated server. After you export a backup, you may choose where to save, copy, transmit, or share the file using functionality provided by your device or other applications. Any third-party service you choose to use for an exported file is governed by that service's own privacy practices. You are responsible for protecting exported backup files, particularly if you store or transmit them using a third-party service. 5. Data Sharing David Burton does not currently receive your Lift Progress workout data and therefore does not sell or share that data with advertisers, data brokers, analytics providers, or other third parties. When you intentionally export or share information using functionality provided by your device, the information may be provided to the destination you select. Such user-directed transfers are outside Lift Progress's control after the information has been provided to that destination. 6. Data Retention and Deletion Lift Progress data remains stored locally on your device unless you modify or delete it, clear the app's stored data through your device where supported, or uninstall the application. Because Lift Progress does not maintain user accounts or a developer-operated database containing your workout data, there is no corresponding Lift Progress server account or cloud record for David Burton to delete. If you create an exported backup, deleting data from the Lift Progress app does not necessarily delete copies that you previously exported or stored elsewhere. You must separately delete those copies from the locations or services where you placed them. 7. Security Lift Progress is designed to minimize privacy risks by keeping app data locally on your device rather than transmitting it to a Lift Progress-operated backend. However, no device, application, or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. The security of locally stored data may also depend on your device, operating-system security settings, device access controls, and how you handle exported backup files. You should use appropriate device security features and take reasonable precautions when storing or sharing exported backups. 8. Notifications Lift Progress may use device notifications for app functionality such as rest-timer alerts. These notifications are generated for app functionality and are not used by Lift Progress for advertising, tracking, or marketing purposes. Your device's operating system controls notification permissions and delivery. 9. Children's Privacy Lift Progress is a general-purpose strength-training and workout application and is not specifically directed to children. Lift Progress does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a Lift Progress-operated server or account system because the app does not currently operate such a system or collect user data remotely. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of exercise applications and strength-training activities as appropriate. 10. Third-Party and Platform Services Lift Progress is distributed through third-party app stores and operates on mobile operating systems provided by third parties, such as Google and Apple. Those companies may independently collect or process information in connection with their app stores, operating systems, device services, purchases, downloads, diagnostics, security features, or other services they provide. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies and is separate from David Burton's handling of information through Lift Progress. Lift Progress does not control the privacy practices of those independent services. 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated if Lift Progress's features, data practices, legal requirements, or distribution methods change. If a future version of Lift Progress introduces features that materially change how user information is collected, transmitted, shared, or otherwise handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate. The effective date and last-updated date at the top of this policy indicate when the current version became effective and was most recently revised. 12.
